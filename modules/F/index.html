Teaching example

Synthetic data, real decisions

This activity uses a fixed dataset of 5,000 simulated adults. It contains no patient data and is not clinical evidence about vaccine effectiveness. The aim is to practice connecting a scientific question, assumptions, estimand, estimator, and interpretation.

Workshop flow

55 minutes, one connected roadmap

  1. 01 0–4 minOrient to Navigator and the reviewable artifact.
  2. 02 4–9 minReveal the crude paradox and predict what adjustment will do.
  3. 03 9–16 minComplete Step 1: define the causal question.
  4. 04 16–23 minComplete Steps 2–3: causal model and observed data.
  5. 05 23–33 minPause at Step 4 and audit identification.
  6. 06 33–39 minComplete Steps 5–6: estimand and estimator.
  7. 07 39–44 minComplete Step 7: sensitivity analysis.
  8. 08 44–49 minReveal and compare the Step 8 results.
  9. 09 49–54 minDefend or revise one Navigator entry.
  10. 10 54–55 minExport the artifact and hand off to Module G.
