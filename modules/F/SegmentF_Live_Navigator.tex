        \item consistency
        \item no interference
      \end{itemize}

          one design change if it fails.}}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.65\textwidth}
      \footnotesize
      \begin{tabular}{>{\bfseries}p{0.25\textwidth}p{0.29\textwidth}p{0.31\textwidth}}
        Assumption & Data can inform & Still requires judgment\\
        \midrule
        Exchangeability & Covariate balance; proxies & Whether key common causes are measured\\[0.38cm]
        Positivity & Fitted score range; support & Whether treatment is possible in every target stratum\\[0.38cm]
        Consistency & Recorded treatment versions & Whether the intervention is well defined\\[0.38cm]
        No interference & Cluster/context clues & Whether one person's treatment affects another
      \end{tabular}

      \vspace{0.16cm}
      {\scriptsize\itshape\color{black!55}
        For this exercise the generator makes the assumptions explicit; the
        observed data alone still cannot certify them.}

\begin{frame}{Step 6: choose an estimator for that target}
  \frametag{TMLE bridge}
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \begin{column}{0.43\textwidth}
      {\normalsize\stepbadge{6}\quad\bfseries Choose the estimator}

      \vspace{0.25cm}
      \footnotesize
      \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Primary:} TMLE for the risk difference
        \item \(\bar Q(A,W)\): outcome regression
        \item \(g(W)\): treatment mechanism
        \item influence-function 95\% CI
      \end{itemize}

      {\scriptsize
        \decisionbox{Boundary question}{
          What can an estimator repair---and what can it never repair?}}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.53\textwidth}
      \footnotesize
      \evidencebox{Outcome-blind work actually completed}{
        No repeated simulation, bias, or RMSE study is included. The adjusted
        outcome result remains hidden until Steps 1--7 are locked.}

      \vspace{0.25cm}
      \evidencebox{Overlap diagnostic}{
        Fitted parametric propensity scores range from 0.0765 to 0.9091.
        This is a diagnostic---not proof of structural positivity.}

      \vspace{0.25cm}
      \evidencebox{Reproducibility convention}{
        Report the version-sensitive TMLE result rounded to
        \(-3.0\) points (95\% CI \(-4.6,-1.5\)).}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
  \note{No repeated simulation or bias/RMSE study is included. Hold the adjusted result until Steps 1--7 are locked.}

% 39--44 minutes
\begin{frame}{Step 7: sensitivity is part of the design}
  \frametag{Checkpoint 4}
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \begin{column}{0.42\textwidth}
      {\large\bfseries
        Prespecify the stress tests before the result.}

      \vspace{0.18cm}
      \footnotesize
      \begin{itemize}
        \item unmeasured confounding
        \item outcome misclassification
        \item selection and censoring
        \item alternative learners and truncation
      \end{itemize}

      {\scriptsize
        \decisionbox{Choose one gap}{
          What analysis or new evidence would make that gap more transparent?}}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.54\textwidth}
      \small
      \evidencebox{Sampling uncertainty}{
        The influence-function interval quantifies uncertainty for the
        statistical estimand under the fitted analysis.}

      \vspace{0.28cm}
      \evidencebox{Causal uncertainty}{
        Separate analyses must address unmeasured confounding, measurement,
        selection, treatment versions, and interference.}

      \vspace{0.28cm}
      {\footnotesize
        \decisionbox{Descriptive tipping point}{
          A \(+3.0\)-point shift would move the TMLE point estimate to the null.
          This is not a formal E-value.}}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}
