# Causal roadmap audit log

Use this log for consequential roadmap decisions, especially entries drafted or
translated with AI. The log records the evidence and edit; it is not a transcript
of every keystroke.

## Disposition rule

- **Accept** — retain the proposed entry because the evidence and wording
  support the intended decision.
- **Correct** — revise the entry while preserving its basic role.
- **Reject** — remove or replace the entry because it is unsupported, unsafe,
  irrelevant, or aimed at the wrong question.

## Minimum evidence check

Before closing an entry, ask:

1. What source, data check, design knowledge, or subject-matter judgment supports
   this decision?
2. What remains uncertain?
3. What observation would make us revise it?
4. If it changes, which downstream roadmap fields, code, tables, or claims must
   change too?
5. Who owns the final decision?

## AI-use note

If AI contributed, record the role it played—such as drafting, translating,
summarizing, challenging, or formatting—and preserve the source evidence used
for human review. Do not treat an AI-generated explanation as evidence for the
scientific claim.
