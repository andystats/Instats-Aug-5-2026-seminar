# Module H facilitator close

This run of show delivers the wrap-up in **4 minutes 30 seconds**, leaving about
30 seconds of recovery time inside the five-minute segment. It is deliberately
simple and does not depend on Module G's estimator or numerical result.

## Before the segment

- Keep the course-materials page open:
  <https://andystats.github.io/Instats-Aug-5-2026-seminar/>.
- Keep the Causal Navigator open in a second tab:
  <https://navigator.tao-rwd.com/>.
- Confirm that the contact slide displays correctly in the webinar share.

## Run of show

| Time | Slide | Facilitator action |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:25 | Module H: Wrap-up | Name the segment and signal that this is a short recap. |
| 0:25–2:25 | One question, one defensible chain | Recap the four links once: target trial, assumptions, estimand/estimator/diagnostics, and bounded interpretation. |
| 2:25–3:55 | Resources and contact | Pause on the course site, Navigator, Kathryn's public company page, and Andy's workshop email. |
| 3:55–4:30 | Thank you | Thank participants, Robert Platt, and Auriane Journet; read the final line once and end. |

## Suggested handoff language

> The estimate was one link in a longer chain. The durable practice is keeping
> the question, assumptions, estimand, analysis, and claim connected in a
> reviewable decision record.

## Recovery options

- **Thirty seconds lost:** shorten the recap examples; keep all four links.
- **One minute lost:** state the recap as one sentence and leave the
  resources/contact slide visible.
- **Course page unavailable:** the repository URL is printed on the slide.
- **Navigator unavailable:** invite participants to return to the archived
  worksheet in Module H.
