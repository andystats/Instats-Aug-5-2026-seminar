# Segment H facilitator close

This run of show delivers the wrap-up in **4 minutes 20 seconds**, leaving about
40 seconds of recovery time inside the five-minute segment. It does not depend
on Module G's dataset, estimator, or numerical result.

## Before the segment

- Keep the Module H participant page open:
  <https://andystats.github.io/Instats-Aug-5-2026-seminar/modules/H/>.
- Confirm the webinar chat is available for the commitment and exit ticket.
- If chat is unavailable, ask participants to write privately; do not spend time
  troubleshooting a feedback tool.

## Run of show

| Time | Slide | Facilitator action |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:45 | The roadmap is the work | Name the question-to-claim chain once. Say that AI can support drafting and challenge, while evidence and people decide. |
| 0:45–2:00 | Before your next model | Return to Module A's exact opening prompt. Give 30 seconds to write a study, first action, and revision trigger. Invite one or two lines in chat if time permits. |
| 2:00–3:00 | Keep the workflow | Open the Module H landing page. Point to blank templates first, the completed roadmap second, and the optional Module G live/technical materials third. |
| 3:00–4:10 | Before you go | Ask participants to post any one exit-ticket response: what they will use, what remains least defensible, or what the teaching team should improve. |
| 4:10–4:20 | Final line | Read the sentence once, pause, and end. |

## Suggested handoff language

> Whatever analysis followed, the estimate was only one link in the chain. The
> durable product is the decision record connecting the question, assumptions,
> estimand, analysis, and claim.

## Recovery options

- **Thirty seconds lost:** skip live examples of commitments; keep the writing
  pause.
- **One minute lost:** show the participant-kit URL, but do not enumerate every
  file.
- **Chat unavailable:** ask participants to save the commitment in their own
  protocol or project repository.
- **Module H page unavailable:** the URL is printed on the slide; the deck and
  templates are also in the seminar repository under `modules/H`.
