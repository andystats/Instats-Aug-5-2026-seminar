# Segment H participant kit

This page is the manifest for the seminar's durable participant materials. Use
the blank templates on a new study; use the pneumonia materials as a worked
teaching example.

## Start with your next study

- [Before Your Next Model commitment card](Next_Study_Commitment.pdf) — make one
  concrete change before analysis begins.
- [Editable commitment card](next-study-commitment.md) — copy the prompts into
  a protocol, issue, or team document.
- [Blank causal roadmap](causal-roadmap-worksheet.md) — record the nine-step
  literature roadmap and its eight-step Navigator mapping.
- [Audit-log template](audit-log-template.md) — accept, correct, or reject
  consequential entries and preserve evidence, uncertainty, and revision
  triggers.
